**DVM-CAR | Automotive Sales & Performance Dashboard**
Transforming Automotive Sales Data into Actionable Business Insights 

 **#Project Overview**
DVM-CAR is an interactive Automotive Sales & Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze automotive sales data and uncover meaningful business insights.
The dashboard analyzes $2.8 billion in sales across 192 car models over 20 years, providing a comprehensive view of automotive business performance through interactive visualizations, dynamic filters, and DAX-powered KPIs.
This project demonstrates the ability to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making and business performance monitoring.
